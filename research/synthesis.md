"# Synthesis: Blended Approach" 
"" 
"Our team's analysis reveals that no single existing solution fully addresses the T6 requirements for a comprehensive NSL-to-text/speech translation system. We blend ideas from all three sources:" 
"" 
"**From Solution 1 (NSL23 Dataset and CNNs):** We adopt the MobileNetV2 architecture with transfer learning as our baseline. The 90.45%% accuracy demonstrates CNNs are effective for NSL character recognition, and transfer learning is crucial for low-resource settings." 
"" 
"**From Solution 2 (Word-Level Transformer):** We adapt LSTM-based sequence modeling for temporal feature recognition. Capturing hand movement patterns is essential for dynamic signs, and LSTMs provide a computationally efficient alternative to Transformers." 
"" 
"**From Solution 3 (Sentence-Level ST-Transformer):** We incorporate MediaPipe for robust hand landmark extraction and the concept of sequence modeling. MediaPipe provides reliable hand tracking, and sequence modeling is essential for any dynamic sign recognition."
