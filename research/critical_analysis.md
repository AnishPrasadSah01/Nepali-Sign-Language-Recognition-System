"# Critical Analysis of Existing NSL Solutions" 
"" 
"## Solution 1: NSL23 Dataset and CNNs (Poudel et al., 2025)" 
"" 
"**Approach:** Poudel et al. introduced the first benchmark dataset for NSL, consisting of 54,000 images spanning 36 gesture classes with 1,500 samples per class. They fine-tuned MobileNetV2 and ResNet50 using transfer learning with a two-phase training strategy." 
"" 
"**Published Metrics:**" 
"- MobileNetV2: 90.45%% accuracy" 
"- ResNet50: 88.78%% accuracy" 
"" 
"**Strengths:**" 
"- Transfer learning leverages ImageNet pre-trained weights, reducing training time and improving generalization in low-resource settings" 
"- Lightweight architecture with 3.5 million parameters" 
"- Comprehensive dataset with variations in backgrounds and lighting conditions" 
"" 
"**Weaknesses (Nepali Context):**" 
"- Static-only recognition - focuses solely on static characters, not dynamic gestures" 
"- Limited to 36 classes (consonants only, excludes 13 vowels)" 
"- Requires significant computational resources" 
"" 
"**Adaptation:** We adopt the MobileNetV2 architecture with transfer learning."
